APA Technical Terms Decoder

Click any card to inspect definitions, strict syntax rules, and examples.

Parenthetical Citation

Author and year are completely enclosed in brackets.

Rule: Place author surname and year in parentheses before sentence punctuation.

Example: Digital learning boosts primary vocabulary (Alwis, 2026).

Narrative Citation

Author name forms part of the natural sentence structure.

Rule: The author's name is incorporated into text; only the publication year stays in parentheses.

Example: Alwis (2026) observed that digital learning boosts vocabulary.

Et al. (And Others)

Used for works with 3 or more authors.

Rule: Abbreviation for "et alia". Use "et al." from the 1st citation for 3+ authors. Put a period after al. but NOT after et.

(Silva et al., 2024)

DOI (Digital Object Identifier)

Unique persistent URL for academic papers.

Rule: Format DOIs strictly as standard URLs starting with https://doi.org/. Do not include "doi:" label or trailing full stops.

https://doi.org/10.1093/elt/ccac041

Sentence Case vs Title Case

Capitalization rules for article vs journal titles.

Sentence Case (Book & Article titles): Capitalize only the 1st word, 1st word after a colon, and proper nouns.
Example: Teaching English: A study in Sri Lanka.

Title Case (Journal titles): Capitalize all major words.
Example: ELT Journal.

Group / Corporate Author

Institutions (e.g., WHO, UNICEF, Ministry of Education).

Rule: Spell out full name on 1st citation with abbreviation in brackets. Use abbreviation on subsequent citations.

1st: (World Health Organization [WHO], 2018)
Next: (WHO, 2018)
